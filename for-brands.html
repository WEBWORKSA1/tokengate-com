FAQ

Before you book

Do my customers need crypto?

No. Embedded wallets let customers sign up with email or social login, and many mints accept cards. The blockchain sits in the background.

How long does a launch take?

A Discord or Shopify gate on an existing collection can ship in 1–2 weeks. A full loyalty program with a new collection typically takes 4–10 weeks.

Are you tied to a vendor?

No. We recommend from the full directory and disclose any referral relationships in your blueprint.

What about legal & tax?

We flag securities, consumer-protection and tax questions early and work alongside your counsel. Nothing here is legal or financial advice.

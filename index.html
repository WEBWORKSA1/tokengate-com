FAQ

Token gating, answered

What is token gating?

Token gating restricts access to content, products, communities or events to wallets that hold a specific token, NFT or credential. The user proves ownership by signing a free message with their wallet.

Does connecting a wallet cost gas or give away my tokens?

No. Legitimate gates ask you to sign a message — not a transaction or approval. If a “gate” asks you to approve spending, stop. See our security checklist.

Which tool is best?

It depends on the door: Collab.Land and Guild lead for Discord/Telegram, Unlock for memberships, Shopify apps for commerce, Lit for developers. Use the Tool Finder.

Can non-crypto users join?

Yes — embedded wallets (email or social login) remove the seed-phrase hurdle, and many platforms support credit-card mints.

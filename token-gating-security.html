Token Gating Security Checklist

6 min read · For holders and builders

Golden rule: verifying ownership never requires approving spending, sending funds, or sharing a seed phrase. A readable signature is enough.

For holders

  • ✅ Only sign plain-text sign-in messages that name the site you're on.
  • ⛔ Reject approve, setApprovalForAll, permit or any transaction during “verification”.
  • ⛔ Never type your seed phrase anywhere — no support team needs it.
  • ✅ Reach bots from the project's official channel, not DMs.
  • ✅ Use a separate “vault” wallet for valuable NFTs and a “hot” wallet for daily use; many gates support delegation.
  • ✅ Periodically review and revoke old approvals with a reputable approval checker.

Unsure about a request? Try our Safety Check.

For builders

  • Use SIWE (EIP-4361) with nonces and expiry to prevent replay.
  • Verify ownership server-side; never trust the client.
  • Re-verify on a schedule and on transfer events (webhooks).
  • Support delegation so users don't expose vault wallets.
  • Rate-limit verification endpoints; add humanity checks for rewards.
  • Publish your official domains and bots; tell users what you will never ask.

If something goes wrong

  1. Disconnect the site and revoke approvals immediately.
  2. Move remaining assets to a fresh wallet.
  3. Warn your community through official channels.
  4. Document transaction hashes for exchanges and investigators.

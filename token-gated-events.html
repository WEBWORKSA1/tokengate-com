Token-Gated Events & NFT Ticketing

7 min read · Events playbook

Token-gated events admit guests based on what their wallet holds — a ticket NFT, a membership pass or a community token. After the event, attendance badges (POAPs) unlock perks for next time.

Three models

  • Holder-only: existing holders get in free — no ticket needed.
  • NFT ticket: sell or airdrop a ticket NFT; resale can be allowed with royalties.
  • Hybrid: public tickets plus holder-only VIP areas, merch or seating.

Check-in workflow

  1. Guest registers with a wallet or email (embedded wallet).
  2. Before the event, the platform issues a signed pass (QR or wallet pass).
  3. At the door, staff scan; the system re-checks ownership in real time.
  4. On entry, guests claim a POAP.
  5. After the event, POAP holders unlock discounts, content or early access.

Tools

Tokenproof for brand activations and check-in, POAP for attendance, Unlock for ticket NFTs, and Guild for pre-event community roles. Compare them in the directory.

Anti-fraud tips

  • Re-verify at the door — screenshots of QR codes aren't proof.
  • Rotate QR codes on a timer.
  • Limit one entry per token per event.

Head-to-head verdicts

Guild vs Collab.Land

Pick Collab.Land if Discord/Telegram roles are all you need and your member count is small — the free tier covers 25 verified members. Pick Guild once you want points, leaderboards, cross-platform roles (GitHub, Google) and a member CRM; flat tiers get cheaper than per-member pricing as you grow.

Unlock vs Lit Protocol

Unlock mints the key (time-based membership NFTs with renewals and wallet passes). Lit enforces conditions on encrypted data. Many stacks use both: Unlock for the membership, Lit for gated files.

Galxe vs Zealy vs Layer3

Galxe for credentials & identity, Zealy for community-first quest boards with USDC rewards, Layer3 for on-chain action campaigns. For contests on TokenGate we pair quests with a gated reward.

No-code vs SDK

No-code ships in a day but locks you to a vendor's UX. SDKs (thirdweb, Alchemy, Moralis, Privy) cost engineering time but give custom rules, analytics and brand control. Rule of thumb: under 5k members, go no-code.

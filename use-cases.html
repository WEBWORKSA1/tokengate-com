12 Token Gating Use Cases

Where token gating creates real value — and the tools to launch each one.

💎

Loyalty programs

Tiered perks by holdings or tenure; customers own their status.
Stack: Unlock, Shopify apps, Privy.

🛍️

Holder-only commerce

Exclusive products, discounts and early drops.
Stack: Shopify gating apps, OpenSea Redeemables.

💬

Private communities

Holder channels in Discord/Telegram.
Stack: Collab.Land, Guild.

🎟️

Events & ticketing

NFT tickets and wallet check-in.
Stack: Tokenproof, POAP, Unlock.

📚

Content & research

Gated articles, reports, videos and downloads.
Stack: Lit Protocol, Unlock + WordPress.

🎓

Courses & cohorts

Course access as a transferable NFT; alumni credentials.
Stack: Unlock, soulbound badges.

🗳️

DAO governance

Token-weighted proposals and votes.
Stack: Snapshot, Safe.

⚙️

SaaS features

Premium features unlocked by a subscription NFT.
Stack: thirdweb, Dynamic, Alchemy.

🎮

Gaming

Items, levels or servers gated to asset holders.
Stack: thirdweb, Helius.

🎁

Airdrops & rewards

Rewards limited to real, active users.
Stack: Galxe, Zealy, Gitcoin Passport.

🤝

Cross-brand partnerships

Reward another community's holders to acquire customers.
Stack: Guild, Shopify apps.

🏨

Real-world perks

Hotel upgrades, lounges, restaurant tables for members.
Stack: Tokenproof, wallet passes.

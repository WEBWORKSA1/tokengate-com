About TokenGate

TokenGate exists to make ownership-based access simple. We research every major token-gating platform, publish vendor-neutral guides and free tools, and connect brands with specialists who can build their gates.

Editorial standards

  • We test tools and cite vendor pricing pages; pricing snapshots are dated and may change.
  • Sponsored content and affiliate links are always labeled.
  • Sponsors never pay for verdicts or rankings.
  • Corrections are welcome and handled within 72 hours.

How we're funded

Display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labeled sponsorships and featured listings, affiliate partnerships, project referrals and reader donations.
